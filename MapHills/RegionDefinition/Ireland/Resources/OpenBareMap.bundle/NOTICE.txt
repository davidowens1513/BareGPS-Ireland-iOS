NOTICE

This directory is the offline map payload for BareGPS Ireland 2.0. It is not a
standalone application. The host app must read local MBTiles, load the bundled
style and serve the local fonts, sprites and GeoJSON without network access.

Required visible attribution in a browsable map:
  Copyright OpenStreetMap contributors

OpenStreetMap-derived databases remain subject to ODbL 1.0. SRTM-derived
terrain is public-domain US government data. Bundled Open Sans glyphs are
covered by Apache License 2.0. See ATTRIBUTION.txt and
LICENSE-APACHE-2.0.txt.

The OpenBareMap map-data version is 1.1.0. The target application release is
BareGPS Ireland 2.0; these are deliberately recorded as separate versions.
