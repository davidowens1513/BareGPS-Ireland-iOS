ATTRIBUTION AND DATA SOURCES

License (ODbL) 1.0.
https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright
https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/

The basemap and the environmental, hiking and island layers contain data
derived from OpenStreetMap. Processing inputs include the Geofabrik Ireland
and Northern Ireland extract dated 2026-08-25. Coastline/land polygons are
derived from OpenStreetMap and were processed by osmdata.openstreetmap.de.
https://download.geofabrik.de/europe/ireland-and-northern-ireland.html
https://osmdata.openstreetmap.de/data/land-polygons.html

Terrain and contours
Hillshade and contour lines were derived from SRTM 1 Arc-Second Global,
Version 3 elevation data. SRTM was produced by NASA and NGA and is distributed
by the USGS; USGS identifies this data as public domain.
https://www.usgs.gov/centers/eros/science/usgs-eros-archive-digital-elevation-shuttle-radar-topography-mission-srtm

Fonts
Map glyphs were generated from Open Sans and distributed in the OpenMapTiles
fonts v2.0 bundle. Open Sans in that bundle is licensed under the Apache
License 2.0. A copy is included as LICENSE-APACHE-2.0.txt.
https://github.com/openmaptiles/fonts

Style and graphics
baregps-style.json and the bundled pattern/crag sprites are the custom
cartography prepared for BareGPS Ireland.
