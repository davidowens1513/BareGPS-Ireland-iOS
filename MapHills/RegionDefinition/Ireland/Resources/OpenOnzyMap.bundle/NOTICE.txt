NOTICE

This is an offline map-data bundle, not a standalone phone application. A
compatible MapLibre-based application or tile server must read the MBTiles,
serve the glyph PBF files, and load style/baregps-style.json.

Required visible attribution in a browsable map:
  Copyright OpenStreetMap contributors

The OpenStreetMap-derived databases remain subject to ODbL 1.0. SRTM-derived
terrain is public-domain US government data. The bundled Open Sans glyphs are
covered by Apache License 2.0. See ATTRIBUTION.txt and
LICENSE-APACHE-2.0.txt.

Source date recorded for the environmental, coastline and hiking overlays:
2026-08-25. The original basemap archive does not contain a source-date field,
so no more specific date is asserted for ireland.mbtiles.
