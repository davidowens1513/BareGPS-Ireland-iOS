# BareGPS Ireland Support

Thank you for using BareGPS Ireland.

BareGPS Ireland helps you explore and log hills across Ireland, record and import GPX tracks, use Go To Hill navigation, and cache map areas for use where signal is limited.

## What the App Does

BareGPS Ireland includes support for:

- viewing hills on a live map;
- filtering and searching hill data;
- marking hills as logged and exporting logged hill data;
- importing, recording, saving, reopening, and sharing GPX tracks;
- showing your live position, heading, elevation, distance, bearing, and grid reference;
- Go To Hill navigation;
- caching map areas for offline use;
- choosing map orientation, units, colours, and other settings.

## Troubleshooting

### Location is not working

Check iPhone Settings and make sure BareGPS Ireland has location permission.

Some features, including live position, Go To Hill navigation, Local 100 from current position, and GPX recording, require location access.

### GPX recording is not continuing in the background

Make sure location access has been granted at a level that allows active recording features to work as expected. Background recording is intended for use during an active walk.

### Imported or saved GPX files are missing

Check the app’s saved GPX files area and any location you used when sharing or exporting files. If you deleted a file from the app or from Files, it may no longer be available.

### Map tiles are not loading

This may be caused by limited or no network connectivity, a temporary provider issue, or lack of cached offline map coverage for the area you are viewing.

### Logged hills or settings appear to have reset

The app stores this information locally on your device. If the app was removed, the device was reset, or local app data was cleared, those items may no longer be available.

## Before You Email

If possible, include:

- your iPhone model;
- your iOS version;
- the version of BareGPS Ireland you are using;
- a short description of the problem;
- the steps needed to reproduce it.

If your issue involves GPX import or export, mention whether the file was recorded in the app or imported from elsewhere.

## Privacy

BareGPS Ireland uses location data only to provide app features such as showing your position, recording GPX tracks, calculating hill navigation information, and supporting current-position features. User data such as logged hills, app settings, GPX recordings, and offline map caches is primarily stored on your device unless you choose to share it.
