# BareGPS Ireland Privacy Policy

***Date: 30 October 2026. BareGPS Ireland version: 2.0.0 OpenBareMap version: 2.0.0***

BareGPS Ireland is an iPhone app for viewing and exploring hills in Ireland, showing your position on a map, navigating to places, logging hills, and importing, recording and sharing GPX tracks. The app can also download the optional OpenBareMap map for use without a network connection.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the app uses, how that information is handled, and the choices available to you.

## 1. Information used by the app

Depending on the features you choose to use, BareGPS Ireland may use or store the following information on your device:

- your location, if you grant location permission;
- GPX files that you import;
- GPX tracks that you record;
- hills that you mark as logged;
- saved map and app preferences;
- downloaded OpenBareMap files; and
- temporary map data used by online map layers.

BareGPS Ireland does not require you to create an account or sign in.

## 2. Location information

If you grant location permission, BareGPS Ireland uses your location to provide app functions such as:

- showing your current position on the map;
- showing coordinates and grid references;
- calculating distance, bearing and related navigation information;
- supporting Go To navigation;
- supporting location-based hill searches and lists; and
- recording GPX tracks.

When you start a GPX recording, the app may use location in the background so that the recording can continue while the app is not on screen or the iPhone is locked.

Location permission is optional. Features that depend on your current position will not work correctly if permission is not granted.

Your location is not used for advertising or cross-app tracking.

## 3. Information stored on your device

BareGPS Ireland is designed so that your app data is primarily stored locally on your iPhone.

- Logged hills and app preferences are stored locally.
- Recorded and imported GPX files are stored locally unless you choose to export or share them.
- Downloaded OpenBareMap files are stored in the app's private Application Support storage.
- Temporary online-map data may be stored locally by the map system.

Other than the network requests described below, the developer does not operate an online BareGPS Ireland account or cloud database containing your logged hills, GPX tracks or app settings.

## 4. OpenBareMap

OpenBareMap is an optional hiking-focused map for BareGPS Ireland. It is an approximately 1 GB download and can be used without cellular or Wi-Fi coverage after it has been installed.

The OpenBareMap files are stored in private app storage on the iPhone. The OpenBareMap storage folder is marked as excluded from normal iCloud backup because the map can be downloaded again.

You can remove OpenBareMap from within BareGPS Ireland by opening **Settings**, finding **OpenBareMap**, tapping **Manage OpenBareMap**, and choosing **Remove Map**.

Removing OpenBareMap removes the downloaded map package. It does not remove your GPX files, logged hills or app settings.

## 5. OpenBareMap downloads and Cloudflare

BareGPS Ireland checks for and downloads OpenBareMap files from:

`downloads.appsbyonzy.dev`

The files are stored and delivered using Cloudflare services, including Cloudflare R2.

When the app checks for or downloads OpenBareMap, Cloudflare may receive the information normally involved in an internet request. This may include:

- your IP address;
- the date and time of the request;
- the requested address or file;
- technical information supplied by the app, iPhone and network connection; and
- diagnostic and security information generated while delivering the request.

These requests are made to check map availability and deliver the files that you request. BareGPS Ireland does not send your GPX tracks, logged hills or app settings to the OpenBareMap download server.

Cloudflare handles information under its own privacy policy. Cloudflare's privacy policy is available at:

https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/

## 6. Wi-Fi and cellular downloads

OpenBareMap downloads are restricted to Wi-Fi by default. You can turn off the **Wi-Fi Only** option in **Manage OpenBareMap** before starting a download if you want to permit cellular data.

The app does not permit OpenBareMap downloads over a constrained network connection, such as a connection restricted by iOS Low Data Mode.

Changing this option affects the network connection allowed for the map download. It does not send your location, GPX tracks, logged hills or app settings to the map server.

## 7. Online maps and external services

Some optional map layers and app links use internet services operated by other organisations. These include, as applicable:

- OpenStreetMap-based map services;
- OpenTopoMap;
- Esri imagery and related imagery services;
- MountainViews.ie hill pages; and
- other map or data providers identified in the app's credits and attribution information.

When you use an online map, imagery layer or external link, the relevant provider may receive standard internet request information such as your IP address, the time of the request, the requested map tiles or webpage, and technical information sent with the request. Requests for map tiles can indicate the general map area being viewed.

Those services operate under their own privacy policies and terms.

MapLibre is used by the app to render maps. OpenBareMap itself is read from the files on your iPhone after installation and does not need to contact its source-data providers during normal offline use.

## 8. GPX imports, recordings and sharing

GPX files that you import or record are stored locally by the app. BareGPS Ireland does not automatically upload those files to the developer.

If you choose to export or share a GPX file, iOS sends it to the person, app or service that you select. The selected destination handles the file under its own privacy practices.

## 9. Contacting support

If you contact support by email, the developer receives your email address, your message and any other information that you choose to include, such as screenshots or diagnostic details.

Support information is used to respond to your request, investigate the reported issue and maintain the app. Do not include precise location information or GPX files unless they are needed for your support request and you are comfortable sharing them.

## 10. Advertising, sale and tracking

BareGPS Ireland does not sell your personal information.

The app does not use your location, GPX tracks, logged hills or app settings for advertising or cross-app tracking.

## 11. Your choices

You can:

- grant, decline or revoke location permission in iOS Settings;
- stop an active GPX recording;
- delete GPX files that you created or imported;
- change or clear app settings and logged-hill information using the app's available controls;
- restrict OpenBareMap downloads to Wi-Fi;
- cancel an active OpenBareMap download;
- remove the installed OpenBareMap package; and
- remove BareGPS Ireland from your iPhone to delete its locally stored app data, subject to iOS backup and restore behaviour.

The OpenBareMap folder is specifically excluded from normal iCloud backup. Other app data may be handled by iOS backup according to your device and iCloud settings.

## 12. Data security

BareGPS Ireland relies on the security and app-sandbox protections provided by iOS for information stored on your device. OpenBareMap downloads use HTTPS, and the app accepts map-file addresses only from its approved download host. Downloaded map files are checked before installation, including file-size, SHA-256 checksum and map-database checks.

No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure. The app is designed to minimise unnecessary collection and transfer of personal information.

## 13. Children

BareGPS Ireland is not directed specifically to children under 13. If a parent or guardian believes that a child has provided personal information through a support request or another direct contact, please contact the developer so the matter can be addressed.

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the app or its services change. The current version will be published on this page, with the updated date shown at the top.
