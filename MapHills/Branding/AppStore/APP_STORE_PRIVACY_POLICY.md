# BareGPS Ireland Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 2026

BareGPS Ireland is an iPhone app for viewing hills in Ireland, showing your position on the map, navigating to hills, logging hills, importing and recording GPX tracks, and optionally caching map areas for offline use.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the app uses, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

## 1. Data We Use

BareGPS Ireland may use the following information on your device:

- Location data, if you grant permission.
- GPX files you import into the app.
- GPX tracks you record in the app.
- Logged hill selections and app settings, such as display preferences and filter choices.
- Offline map cache data saved for your own use.

## 2. How Location Data Is Used

If you allow location access, BareGPS Ireland uses your location to:

- Show your current position on the map.
- Calculate distance, bearing, elevation, and grid reference information.
- Support Go To Hill navigation.
- Record GPX tracks while you are walking.
- Continue active GPX recording in the background when that feature is in use.
- Support features such as Local 100 based on your current position.

Location access is optional, but some app features will not work correctly without it.

## 3. Where Your Data Is Stored

BareGPS Ireland is designed so that your data is primarily stored on your device.

- Logged hills are stored locally on your device.
- App settings are stored locally on your device.
- Recorded GPX files are stored locally on your device unless you choose to share or export them.
- Imported GPX files and offline map cache data are stored locally on your device as needed for app features.

## 4. Data Sharing

BareGPS Ireland does not sell your personal data.

The app only shares data when you choose to do so, for example:

- when you export or share a GPX file;
- when you open an external hill page on MountainViews.ie;
- when you contact support by email;
- when the app loads online map tiles or related map content from third-party providers.

When you use online maps or external links, those third-party services may receive standard technical information such as your IP address and the request needed to deliver the map or webpage. Depending on the feature used, map requests may reflect the area you are viewing on the map.

## 5. Third-Party Services and Data Sources

BareGPS Ireland uses or links to third-party services and data sources, including:

- MountainViews.ie for hill information and hill pages.
- MapLibre for map rendering.
- OpenStreetMap contributors.
- OpenTopoMap.
- Esri and other imagery/map providers used by the app.

These third parties may have their own privacy policies and terms.

## 6. No Account Required

BareGPS Ireland does not require you to create an account to use the app.

## 7. Children

BareGPS Ireland is not directed specifically to children under 13. If a parent or guardian believes that personal information has been provided through support email or another direct contact channel, please contact us so we can address the issue.

## 8. Your Choices

You can:

- decline or revoke location permission in iOS Settings;
- delete GPX files you created or imported;
- clear logged hills from within the app;
- remove the app to delete app data stored on your device, subject to any iOS backup behavior.

## 9. Security

BareGPS Ireland relies on the security protections provided by iOS for on-device data storage. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but the app is designed to minimize unnecessary collection of personal information.

## 10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version should be published wherever the app’s App Store privacy link is hosted.
